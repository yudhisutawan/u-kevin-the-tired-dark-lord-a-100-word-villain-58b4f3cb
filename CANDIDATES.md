## Kandidat Hermes (8)

### H1 — Hermes — admin pacts & chamomile tea

> Another hero. Sword shining. Shield polished to a pathetic, blinding gleam. Do you have any idea what time it is, boy? No, of course you don't. You've had eight hours of uninterrupted sleep in a feather bed. Meanwhile, I have spent four centuries calibrating ley-lines, managing insolent wraiths who refuse to dust the catacombs, and listening to the high priest whine about tallow budgets. My dark pacts expire on Tuesday. I have a migraine behind my third eye. Just take the cursed amulet, step on the trapdoor, and let me finish my chamomile tea in silence.

### H2 — Hermes — broken door & soul-tithe ledgers

> You kicked my heavy oak door off its iron hinges. Again. Do you know how hard it is to source blacksmiths in the Ashlands? Everything is obsidian or rusted scrap. You come in here shouting about prophecy, bloodlines, and dawn, while I have been awake since the First Age balancing ledgers of soul-tithes. My femur-throne is lopsided. The portcullis squeaks. I don't even want the realm anymore; I want five minutes of quiet where nobody asks me to curse a harvest. Strike me down if you must, but wipe your boots.

- Kata: 91 | Karakter: 513

### H3 — Hermes — goblin-tax & rescheduled eclipses

> Put the broadsword down, Kendrick. Your posturing is giving me vertigo. Look at these scrolls. Four thousand years of eternal empire, and every single goblin clan is behind on their goblin-tax. The abyss is drafting new safety regulations. Do you think I enjoy plunging the continent into shadow? It requires rescheduling three eclipses and dealing with dampness in the lower dungeons. I am so tired of conquering. Take the crown. Wear it. Try paying mercenaries in screaming souls when the treasury is empty. Enjoy the paperwork, champion.

- Kata: 87 | Karakter: 540

### H4 — Hermes — unionized dread knights & calcium

> Look at you. Brandishing that glowing heirloom like it hasn't been done six thousand times before. Do you know how exhausting it is to maintain an aura of absolute dread when your joints pop every time you rise from the throne? The dread knights unionized last moon. The dragon went on strike over sulfur pensions. I haven't slept a proper century since the third epoch. If you are here to vanquish me, please be quick about it. Aim for the heart, avoid the ribs—calcium replacement is terribly expensive these days.

- Kata: 90 | Karakter: 516

### H5 — Hermes — spire innkeeper & lichen cease-fire

And yet, every Tuesday, some bright-eyed squire climbs seven thousand stairs just to discuss the balance of light and dark. My knees ache, squire. The gargoyles leak when it rains. I spent all morning negotiating a cease-fire with the lichens in the cellar. I am not destroying your village today. I am going to bed. If you touch my tapestry, I will turn your armor inside out. Otherwise, close the heavy oak door on your way out.

### H6 — Hermes — drafts & unmopped dungeon floor

I have spent three hundred years perfecting the eternal gloom over this valley, and do you know what I get? Drafts. Eternal drafts and ungrateful skeleton janitors. I do not care about your sacred bloodline or your shining destiny. I care that the dungeon floor hasn't been mopped since the Second Interregnum. Strike me or leave me, boy, but keep your voice down.

### H7 — Hermes — disbanded Dark Army logistics

> The Dark Army is disbanded. The obsidian gates are unlocked. Why are you still standing there with your legendary mace raised? I surrendered to exhaustion at midnight. Do you have any conception of what managing immortal legions entails? The logistics alone—securing blood-tithes, feeding hellhounds grain-free rations, updating cursed epitaphs when ghosts misspell their own names. I am seventy centuries old and my shoulders feel like granite. Take the dark orb, sell it to the guild, and let me stare at the wall in peace.

- Kata: 84 | Karakter: 525

### H8 — Hermes — twitching eye & brimstone audits

> You draw your blade with such dramatic flair. How thrilling. Truly. Meanwhile, my left eye has been twitching since the Siege of Oakhaven. Do you imagine ruling the underworld is all lightning and brimstone? It is budget audits for brimstone refineries. It is listening to banshees complain about acoustics. I have not felt warmth in an epoch, and frankly, I don't care if the sun rises over your pathetic kingdom anymore. Light it on fire yourself if you're so energetic. I am going to lie face down on the cold floor.

## Kandidat Lokal (8)

### L1 — Lokal — ledger of the damned, late eclipse

> Another hero. Do you knock? No. None of you knock. Four hundred years I have kept the ledger of the damned balanced, and you arrive at half past the witching hour, tracking mud across a floor the wraiths mopped twice. The high priest wants a famine by Friday. The grave-worms want a raise. My third eye has been twitching since the Siege of Oakhaven, and the eclipse I scheduled three centuries ago is — ah. There it is. Late. Take the amulet. Take the crown. Take the whole blighted ledger. Just sign the receipt, hero. Even damnation has paperwork.

- Kata: 99 | Karakter: 550

### L2 — Lokal — arthritic dragon & nap schedule

> You want the dragon. Of course you want the dragon. Everyone wants the dragon. I hatched him from an egg the size of a millstone, taught him to breathe fire without singeing the tapestries, and now he is four hundred years old, arthritic, and afraid of his own shadow. I spend my mornings rubbing liniment into his wings. My afternoons, haggling with goblin apothecaries over sulfur. You think I plot the burning of kingdoms? I plot his nap schedule. Strike me down, and the beast starves. Walk away, and — hmm. Actually. Take him. He snores.

- Kata: 96 | Karakter: 542

### L3 — Lokal — retired Dark Lord runs an inn

> Sit down, hero. No — not on the good chair. That one is for the plague doctor, and he has been dead since Tuesday. I have run this inn at the edge of the marsh for three hundred years, and still every bright-eyed boy walks in asking for the Dark Lord. I am the Dark Lord. I also do the laundry. Upstairs is damp, the stew is mostly turnip, and the cellar — the cellar is where I keep the old throne. You want a quest? Wash a dish. The last hero who did is still here. He never left.

- Kata: 100 | Karakter: 482

### L4 — Lokal — necromancer mother & 3,000 skeletons

> Put that sword away before someone loses an eye. I have raised three thousand skeletons this century and not one of them stays buried past supper. They wander off. They lose their femurs. Last week one of them tried to unionize. I am not plotting the fall of your kingdom, boy — I am trying to get my children to bed. The grave-soil is damp, the shovels are rusted, and the high priest keeps sending me invoices for funerals I already performed. Go on. Slay me. Then explain to three thousand restless dead why their mother isn't coming home.

- Kata: 99 | Karakter: 542

### L5 — Lokal — villain sealed inside a mirror

> Do not smash the mirror. Please. Not again. I have been sealed inside this glass for six hundred years, and every decade some bright-eyed hero mistakes me for a decoration. I can see the whole kingdom from here, you know. I watch the harvests fail. I watch the tax collectors grow fat. I planned my return once — an eclipse, a choir of the damned, a throne of blackened bone — and then I realized the view from in here is better. Go on, then. Break it. I will simply move into yours. You will make a lovely frame.

- Kata: 99 | Karakter: 513

### L6 — Lokal — soul contracts in triplicate

> You are early. The appointment was for midnight, and it is — let me look — barely dusk. Four thousand years of empire, and still every hero arrives before the ink is dry. I have contracts to sign, boy. Souls do not damn themselves; somebody has to file them in triplicate, and the abyss has started auditing. Your name is already on the form, third from the bottom, right beneath the goat farmer. The waiting room is full of paladins, and the quill writes in blood. So. Shall we do this? Sign here. Initial. And mind the seal — it bites.

- Kata: 101 | Karakter: 537

### L7 — Lokal — ninth hero, loyalty-card apocalypse

> Ninth hero this month. Ninth. I have started stamping a card — collect six seals, the seventh apocalypse is free. Do you know what the last eight did? They monologued. At me. In my own throne room. I have ruled the Ashlands for four centuries, boy, and I cannot remember the last time I slept past dawn without some chosen one rattling the portcullis. My dread knights have gone home. The dragon filed for retirement. The moat smells. The torches keep going out. So go ahead. Raise the sword. Just — could you make it quick? The kettle is on.

- Kata: 100 | Karakter: 542

### L8 — Lokal — already won, throne is meetings

> You are too late, hero. I won. Three years ago. The banners are mine, the treasury is mine, and the crown — the crown is currently holding down a stack of unpaid invoices. The sceptre doubles as a letter opener. The orb is a paperweight. Ruling an empire, it turns out, is mostly meetings. Tuesdays with the goblin clans. Thursdays with the wraiths, who never stop complaining about the damp. I have a scheme, yes. It is to survive until Friday without another prophecy. Take the throne, if you want it. The chair is warm. The minutes are yours.

- Kata: 99 | Karakter: 545
